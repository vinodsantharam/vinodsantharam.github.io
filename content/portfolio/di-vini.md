## The product

An app to record and keep wine tasting notes, on iOS and Android.

## What I did

- Set up the development environment, libraries and project structure.
- Integrated Firebase for storage and Google Analytics for tracking.
- Built custom components such as an expander, an input and a toggle button.
- Automated releases with Fastlane.
